# Wosnitza, Bockemühl, Dübbert, Scholz & Büschges 2013

**Citation.** Wosnitza A, Bockemühl T, Dübbert M, Scholz H, Büschges A (2013). "Inter-leg coordination in the control of walking speed in *Drosophila*." *Journal of Experimental Biology* 216(3):480–491. DOI 10.1242/jeb.078139.
URL: https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/216/3/480/11966/

**Method.** High-speed video, **500 fps**, 200 µs shutter. Four strains chosen to span the widest possible speed range in one species. 2,089 steps total; n = 15 trials for wtCS.

## Verified (single WebFetch extraction of the JEB full text — re-check against PDF before publication)

Speed ranges:

| Strain | mm/s | BL/s |
|---|---|---|
| wt Canton-S | 11–32 | 5–16 |
| wt Berlin | 11–34 | 5–15 |
| w1118 | 4–31 | 2–15 |
| w1118, Tbh^nM18 | 3–14 | 1.5–7 |

- **Core claim: "*Drosophila* controls its walking speed almost exclusively via step frequency."**
- Step amplitude ≈ **0.5 body length** (wtCS, wtBerlin); slightly under 0.5 BL for w1118. Correlation with speed weak, R² = 0.03–0.17.
- Cycle period vs speed: hyperbolic, R² = 0.76 (wtCS).
- Swing duration vs cycle period: R² = 0.37 (wtCS). Typical swing durations **20–40 ms**.
- **Tripod coordination strength (TCS) = t₂/t₁**, where t₁ = time from the earliest swing onset to the latest swing termination in a tripod group, t₂ = the interval during which all three legs of the group swing simultaneously. TCS = 1 is perfect tripod.
  - above **10 BL/s**: TCS reaches maximal values up to **0.85**
  - below 10 BL/s: TCS spans **0.02–0.8** (highly variable)
- Tetrapod/wave-like coordination appears below ~**5 BL/s**.
- Within a tripod at high speed: front leg swings first, midleg ~**15 deg** of cycle later, hindleg ~**15 deg** after that (≈0.042 cycle per step in the metachronal chain).
- **Duty factor was not calculated in this study.**
