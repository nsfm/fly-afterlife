# Kuan et al. 2020 — X-ray holographic nano-tomography of a whole Drosophila front leg
### *** THE definitive per-leg mechanosensory inventory ***

**Citation:** Kuan AT, Phelps JS, Thomas LA, Nguyen TM, Han J, Chen C-L, Azevedo AW, Tuthill JC,
Funke J, Cloetens P, Pacureanu A, Lee W-CA (2020). *Dense neuronal reconstruction through X-ray
holographic nano-tomography.* **Nature Neuroscience 23:1637–1643.** doi:10.1038/s41593-020-0704-9.
PMC8354006, PMID 32929244. Preprint doi:10.1101/653188.
**Status: VERIFIED — PMC full text + Supplementary Information PDF (MOESM1) read directly.
Supplementary Data Table 3 transcribed below verbatim.**

Dataset: one adult *Drosophila* **front leg**, 10 high-res XNH scans at 50–75 nm voxels + 2
overview scans at 160 nm. Resolution achieved for axon tracing: 150–200 nm.

## DERIVED TOTALS FOR ONE DROSOPHILA FRONT LEG (my arithmetic from Table 3)
- **Proprioceptors only** (hair plates 37 + campaniform 33 + FeCO 152 + stretch 2 + strand 1)
  = **225**. This is the source of Pratt et al. 2026's "the fruit fly's front leg contains over 200
  proprioceptive sensory neurons."
- **All mechanosensory neurons** = 225 + 233 bristles = **≥458 per front leg**, and that is a
  **lower bound** (tarsal bristles + distal-half-of-tibia bristles + tarsal CS not imaged).
  With Elabbady et al. 2026's 409 bristle axons reconstructed in FANC from the left front leg,
  a fuller estimate is **225 proprioceptors + ~409 bristles ≈ 634 mechanosensory neurons per
  front leg.** (Mixing two datasets/individuals — treat as an estimate, not a measurement.)

## CROSS-CHECK AGAINST PRATT et al. 2026's "214 neurons in 42 hair plates" (all six legs)
From Pratt's Fig. S1A the hair plates present per leg are:
- Front: CxHP3, CxHP4, CxHP8, TrHP1, TrHP5, TrHP6, TrHP7, TiHP3 = **8 plates, 37 neurons**
- Middle: CxHP4, CxHP8, TrHP3, TrHP5, TrHP6, TrHP7, TiHP2, TiHP3 = **8 plates, 38 neurons**
- Hind: CxHP4, CxHP8, TrHP5, TrHP6, TrHP7 = **5 plates, 30 neurons**
- Per side 8+8+5 = 21 plates; **×2 sides = 42 plates ✓ exactly matches Pratt's figure.**
- Neurons per side 37+38+30 = 105; ×2 = **210**, vs Pratt's stated **214** — a 4-neuron gap.
  Either I misread one label in the schematic, or one plate's count departs from the naming rule.
  **Flagging the discrepancy rather than papering over it.**

## AXON DIAMETERS — useful for conduction-delay modelling (VERIFIED)
| Axon class | Diameter |
|---|---|
| CoHP3 sensory axons | **1380 ± 20 nm** |
| CoHP4 sensory axons | **1140 ± 240 nm** |
| CoHP8 sensory axons | **1030 ± 90 nm** |
| Coxa-innervating motor axons (n = 5) | **1140 ± 130 nm** |
| Thorax-innervating motor axons (n = 2) | **1880 and 2150 nm** |
| Chordotonal + bristle axons | too narrow to trace at 150–200 nm resolution |

"we found systematic variation in the axon diameter of different sensory neuron types, suggesting
that some types of sensory signals (namely those from **coxal hair plates and trochanteral
campaniform sensilla**) are conducted to the VNC **faster or more reliably** than others."
⇒ **Hair plate and trochanteral CS axons are the fast channel; chordotonal and bristle axons the
slow one.** No conduction velocities in m/s are measured in this paper.

## Other numbers worth keeping
- Femur contains **97 muscle fibres** — "nearly triple that reported previously using fluorescence
  microscopy."
- **6 long tendon muscle (LTM) fibres in the femur**, organised as a group of 2 and a group of 4,
  attaching to the long tendon at different points.
- **100 neurons** reconstructed entering the VNC via the main leg nerve, classified as motor vs
  sensory subtypes by axon calibre and branching pattern.
- Coxal hair plate clusters "are positioned differently within the joint, **implying that they are
  sensitive to different joint angle ranges**" — the anatomical basis of Pratt's limit-detector result.
