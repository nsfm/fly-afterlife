# Lee et al. 2025 — Divergent proprioceptive vs exteroceptive circuits, Drosophila leg

**Citation:** Lee S-YJ, Dallmann CJ, Cook A, Tuthill JC, Agrawal S (2025). *Divergent neural circuits
for proprioceptive and exteroceptive sensing of the Drosophila leg.* **Nature Communications
16:4105.** doi:10.1038/s41467-025-59302-3. PMC12048489. Published 2025-05-02. PMID 40316553.
Preprint: bioRxiv 2024.04.23.590808.
**Status: VERIFIED — full PDF read.**

Datasets: **FANC** (female adult nerve cord) + **FAFB/FlyWire** (brain, public release v783).
Reconstructed the front **left (T1L)** leg's FeCO and everything downstream.

## The central claim
FeCO subtypes split into two functionally distinct pathways:
- **Claw (position) + hook (movement) → local VNC leg motor circuits ⇒ PROPRIOCEPTIVE.**
- **Club (vibration) → integrated across legs, ascends to brain mechanosensory regions ⇒
  EXTEROCEPTIVE.**

## Numbers (all verified)
- FeCO = **~150 excitatory (cholinergic) sensory neurons**, **5 subtypes**:
  (1) claw-extension, (2) claw-flexion → **tibia position**;
  (3) hook-extension, (4) hook-flexion → **directional tibia movement**;
  (5) club → **bidirectional tibia movement + low-amplitude (<1 µm), high-frequency vibration**.
- **80 FeCO axons reconstructed** from T1L = **~50% of each subtype**. For comparison, MANC v1.2.1
  had only **22** T1L FeCO axons, many incomplete.
- **5 club axons** from T1L send an ascending projection to the brain.
- Each FeCO neuron contacts **21.1 ± 1.1** (mean ± s.e.m.) distinct postsynaptic partners —
  postsynaptic connectivity is sparse.
- All FeCO axons have more presynaptic than postsynaptic sites; pre/post sites are intermingled,
  no separate zones.
- Synapse threshold for calling a connection: **≥4 synapses**; text notes a 3–4 synapse threshold
  mitigates false positives.
- No strong evidence for functional specialisation of the hook/claw sub-branches — most postsynaptic
  neurons receive input from multiple branches.

## Cross-class integration — relevant to hair plates and campaniform sensilla
Fig. 2E heat-map asks, for each FeCO subtype, what fraction of its postsynaptic partners ALSO
receive input from: other FeCO neurons, **campaniform sensilla (CS) axons**, **hair plate (HP)
axons**, **bristle axons**, or non-leg somatosensory axons (neck CO, wing).
> *"the VNC neurons that receive input from claw and hook axons also receive input from other leg
> proprioceptors, such as hair plate and campaniform sensilla neurons. In contrast, the VNC neurons
> that receive input from club axons receive little input from leg proprioceptors, and instead
> receive sizeable input from somatosensory neurons from the neck and wing."*
> *"claw and hook axons shared a larger number of downstream partners with CS or HP neurons than
> with other FeCO subtypes."*
⇒ **In a sim: hair-plate, campaniform and claw/hook FeCO signals converge on the same second-order
premotor population. Club is a separate, brain-bound channel.**
(The paper does **not** state how many HP or CS axons were in its comparison set.)

## Hemilineage routing
- Club-dominated postsynaptic hemilineages: **0A/0B, 1B, 8B, 9A, 10B, 23B**. Of these only 1B and 9A
  get any claw input, and weakly (~6% and 0.5% of total FeCO input respectively).
- Club and hook-flexion share 1B, 9A, 23B. All other identified hemilineages are claw/hook-dominated
  with zero club input.
- Club connectivity is **dorsoventrally organised into sub-clusters** consistent with a putative
  **tonotopic map** of tibia vibration frequency.
- Club axons connect indirectly to escape circuitry: **LTM motor neurons** (long tendon muscle, leg
  freezing) and the **peripherally synapsing interneuron (PSI)** (take-off).

## Explicit negative / species note (useful)
> *"Many insect species also possess **subgenual organs**, specialized vibration sensors in the
> tibia, but **flies lack these sensory structures**."* ⇒ in Drosophila, **club FeCO neurons are the
> substrate-vibration detector.**

## Caveats the authors state
- Leg sensory neurons degenerate during dissection, giving darker cytoplasm and fragmented membranes
  ⇒ poor automatic segmentation. This is why only ~50% of FeCO axons could be reconstructed and why
  Pratt et al. 2026 had to re-reconstruct hair plate axons in FANC instead of using MANC.
- FANC is a **female** VNC; club circuitry could be sexually dimorphic (courtship relevance).
