## Claims and numbers
- **"Flies predominantly employ a modified tripod gait that only changes marginally with speed."** This is the strongest anti-gait-transition position in the literature.
- "The stance duration is inversely proportional to speed. The swing duration also changes with speed but to a smaller extent than the changes in stance duration."
- Ordering within the tripod: "The front leg of the tripod leads the middle leg that in turn leads the back leg", with "normalized delays between different legs consistent with that of a single gait."
- Tripod geometry parameters: **L** = anterior-posterior spread of the tripod; **r_m** = height of the centre of mass at mid-stance. **"The L/r_m ratio varies between 1 and 2" in flies.**
- Mechanism of speed control: **"The change in the stiffness of the spring is mediated by the change in tripod shape rather than a change in stiffness of individual legs."** The fly is treated as a spring-loaded inverted pendulum whose effective stiffness is set by tripod geometry.
