## Velocity ranges (the binning grid used throughout)
- Forward/translational **v_T: −0.6 to 3.2 cm/s (−6 to +32 mm/s)**, in 0.09 cm/s increments.
- Rotational **|v_R| < 450 °/s**, in 25 °/s increments.
- Sideways/side-slip **|v_S| < 0.94 cm/s (9.4 mm/s)**, in 0.063 cm/s increments.

## Behavioural structure
- **15 submodes** identified by iterative ICA, grouped into **5 modes** by Markov modelling ("five submode groups, or modes, provided a sufficient model").
- First-order Markov dependence **ρ = 0.271** (27.1% of uncertainty explained); second order negligible, **ρ₂ = 0.015**.
- Markov model predictive horizon **τ_P = 4.3 ± 0.55 s**, exceeding the ~1 s velocity autocorrelation decay.
- Divergence of nearby trajectories plateaus for up to **~200 ms**.

## Turning cadence
- **Inter-peak interval between successive rotational-velocity peaks: 250 ± 110 ms.**
