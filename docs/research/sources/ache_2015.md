# Ache & Dürr 2015 — hair-field afferent encoder (filter cascade, fully parameterised)

**Citation.** Ache JM, Dürr V (2015). "A Computational Model of a Descending Mechanosensory
Pathway Involved in Active Tactile Sensing." *PLoS Computational Biology* **11**(7):e1004263.
DOI 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004263. Published 9 July 2015. Open access.
Read in full from the PLOS printable PDF.

Organ: stick insect (*Carausius morosus*) antennal **scape-pedicel (Sc-Pd) hair fields**;
afferent parameters tuned to match **cockroach** antennal hair field sensilla recordings.
Not a leg organ, not *Drosophila* — but the cleanest fully specified joint-angle → spike-
train encoder available, and the structure ports directly to leg hair plates.

## Structure (quotes)

- Hair fields collapsed into **two hair rows, one on each side of the joint**;
  **20 hairs per row** ("a conservative estimate of the average number of hairs in the stick
  insect"); ⇒ 2 × 20 = **40 afferents**.
- "The spacing of hairs was assumed such that the **number of deflected hairs varied
  linearly with antennal joint angle**." Dorsal hairs deflected only for Sc-Pd angle > 0°,
  ventral only < 0°. At rest (0°) no hairs deflected ⇒ no afferent activity.
- "the deflection angle of each hair ... was assumed to **vary linearly with the Sc-Pd joint
  angle within the hair's sensitivity range**."
- Activation = LPF(hair angle)·w_LP + HPF(hair angle)·w_HP, then normalise, then subtract a
  constant Offset. "The resulting activation function can be viewed as the hypothetical
  membrane potential of the sensory afferent."
- Filters: "linear **first-order** high- and low-pass filters with the time constant (tau)
  as the only variable."
- Noisy spike generator (verbatim):
  ```
  spike(t) = 1  if  act(t) · dS · Rmax > rand ;  otherwise spike(t) = 0
  ```
  rand ~ U(0,1). "dS was kept fixed at 1 ms, only Rmax was adjusted."
  "Spikes cannot be elicited at activation levels below zero. For activation levels between
  zero and one, the likelihood of a spike to be elicited at a given Rmax rises linearly."
- **Absolute refractory period 3 ms** per model afferent.

## Table 1 — hair field afferent parameters (verbatim)

| N (hairs) | Range | Deflect. | LPF (tau/w) | HPF (tau/w) | Offset | Norm. | Rmax | dS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 2.5° | slope 1 | 10 ms / 2 | 30 ms / 20 | 35 | 100 | 300 1/s | 0.001 s |

Each hair's sensitivity range spans **2.5°**; 20 hairs × 2.5° = 50° per row ⇒ ±50° working
range. The high-pass branch is weighted **10×** the low-pass branch (20 vs 2) — that ratio
is what makes the afferent phasic-tonic with a strong velocity component.

Validated behaviour (Fig 4): "the **transient activity** of the sensory afferents during
ramp-and-hold stimuli was **proportional to the deflection velocity**, while the
**sustained activity** was **proportional to the deflection amplitude**."
Deflection velocities tested: **25, 50, 90, 150, 250 °/s**.

## Table 2 — descending interneuron (DIN) variants (verbatim)

| DIN type | LPF 1 (tau) | Wd | Wv | LPF 2 (tau/w) | HPF (tau/w) | Offset | rectify | Norm. | Rmax |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SP v/d (simple position) | 5 ms | 0/1 | 1/0 | — | — | — | — | 4 | 10 |
| Ex.DP (dynamic extreme position) | 5 ms | 1 | 1 | 50/2 | 40/20 | −0.2 | — | 20 | 100 |
| ON (velocity, ON-type) | 5 ms | 1 | 1 | 50/2 | 40/20 | −0.2 | abs | 20 | 120 |
| DP v/d (dynamic position) | 5 ms | 0/1 | 1/0 | 50/2 | 40/20 | −0.2 | abs | 20 | 100 |
| OFF (velocity, OFF-type) | 5 ms | 1 | 1 | 50/2 | 40/20 | +1 | −abs | 40 | 30 |

Wd, Wv = weights on the dorsal and ventral afferent pools. "Rectification was not necessary
for simple position-sensitive and dynamic extreme position-sensitive DINs."

## Why it matters here

Inputs are exactly what a physics sim gives (a scalar joint angle per DoF); cost is two
first-order filters plus a Bernoulli draw per afferent; and it produces a *population* with
range fractionation, which is what the FeCO claw array and the leg hair plates actually are.
The successor (van der Veen et al. 2025) says it explicitly: "In comparison with an earlier
model (Ache and Dürr 2015), our present model **replaces filter blocks by a SNN**."
