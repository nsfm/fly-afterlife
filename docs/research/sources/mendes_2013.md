# Mendes, Bartos, Akay, Márka & Mann 2013

**Citation.** Mendes CS, Bartos I, Akay T, Márka S, Mann RS (2013). "Quantification of gait parameters in freely walking wild type and sensory deprived *Drosophila melanogaster*." *eLife* 2:e00231. DOI 10.7554/eLife.00231. (Correction: eLife 2:e00565.)
URL: https://elifesciences.org/articles/00231 · figures: https://elifesciences.org/articles/00231/figures

**Method.** Frustrated total internal reflection (fTIR) imaging of footprints in freely walking flies. 71 wild-type videos analysed. 220 metachronal waves measured.

## Verified numbers (read from eLife full text and figure legends)

- Walking speed range **7.2–44.7 mm/s**; "28 mm/s as the most representative speed"; a dip in occupancy around ~20 mm/s.
- Speed bins: slow ≤19.9 mm/s, medium 20–33.9 mm/s, fast ≥34 mm/s.
- Step period inversely proportional to speed, reaching **a steady state of about ~60 ms** at the fastest speeds (≈16 Hz).
- Swing duration "remains mostly constant"; stance duration inversely proportional to speed; at the fastest speeds "durations of both swing and stance phases equalize" (⇒ ~30 ms each, duty factor →0.5).
- Step length increases almost linearly with speed.
- Metachronal lag, slow flies: **HLagF = 0.505 × P_H + 27.6 ms** (tetrapod signature). Fast flies: **HLagF ≈ Period** (tripod signature).
- Gait occupancy, Table 1 (% of frames):

| Gait | slow | medium | fast |
|---|---|---|---|
| tripod | 31.37 | 51.63 | 64.98 |
| tetrapod | 25.45 | 15.90 | 7.26 |
| pentapod | 30.15 | 14.37 | 13.33 |

- Footprint geometry: "At faster speeds, for all legs, AEP coordinates are shifted anteriorly, while PEP coordinates are shifted posteriorly." Midlegs shift laterally at higher speed; hindlegs move closer to the body.
- **AEP clustering < PEP clustering in 61 of 71 videos (86%)** — foot placement at touchdown is more precise than at lift-off.
- Stance linearity index falls (straighter stance traces) with speed and plateaus above ~34 mm/s.

## Sensory-deprived genotypes (nan³⁶ᵃ; 5-40-Gal4 leg > UAS-TNT)
- walk slower, but retain a typical tripod gait
- **increased step length** — from overreaching (more anterior AEP) *and* delayed swing onset (more posterior PEP)
- increased stance linearity (wobblier), larger footprint-clustering variability, decreased footprint alignment
- swing and stance durations both increased ⇒ longer period
- **swing speed minimally affected** ⇒ motor neurons/muscle presumed functional
- tripod index decreased in slow flies only; preserved at speed. "Flies may be less dependent on sensory feedback when they walk fast."
