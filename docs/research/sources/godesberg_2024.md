# Godesberg, Bockemühl & Büschges 2024

**Citation.** Godesberg V, Bockemühl T, Büschges A (2024). "Natural variability and individuality of walking behavior in *Drosophila*." *Journal of Experimental Biology* **227**(22):jeb247878. DOI 10.1242/jeb.247878. PMID 39422060. Epub 2024 Nov.

Extraction: automated read of the JEB page **[V-fetch]**.

**Why it matters.** The largest single-lab *Drosophila* gait dataset with per-individual statistics — it answers "how much of the spread in the published gait numbers is between-fly rather than within-fly?"

## Dataset
- **103 flies recorded, 88 included in the PCA.**
- **36,942 straight-walking step cycles**; "more than 36,000 steps for each leg"; median **242 step cycles per fly**.
- Camera **200 Hz**, 400 µs shutter, 1000 × 1000 px at **33.3 px/mm**.
- Inclusion threshold **≥2 BL s⁻¹**. Main analysis band **5–7 BL s⁻¹**; comparison bands 2–4 (slow) and 8–10 BL s⁻¹ (fast).

## Results
- **Tripod coordination strength (TCS) is "predominantly found in the range between 0.5 and 0.75"**, over 30,216 instances of two complete consecutive tripod cycles. Compare Wosnitza 2013, which reports TCS up to 0.85 above 10 BL/s — Godesberg's band sits at moderate speed and is a better central estimate for typical walking.
- Kinematic variability decomposes into **five principal components accounting for ~80%** of the variability: **PC1 30.9%, PC2 14.7%, PC3 12%, PC4 11%, PC5 9.5%**.
- 2,640 step cycles used in the PCA.
